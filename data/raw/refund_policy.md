# Refund Policy

## Overview

ShopEase is committed to processing refunds quickly and fairly. This document outlines how refunds work, timelines, and eligibility.

## Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund if:

- Your return is approved under our [Returns Policy](returns_policy.md).
- You received a **wrong item** (different from what you ordered).
- Your item arrived **damaged or defective**.
- Your order was **lost in transit** (tracked shipments only).
- ShopEase cancelled your order before shipping.

## Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** whenever possible.

| Payment Method | Refund Method | Estimated Time |
|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| Credit/Debit Card | Card refund | 5–10 business days |
| PayPal | PayPal balance | 3–5 business days |
| ShopEase Store Credit | Store credit | 1–2 business days |
| Bank Transfer / ACH | Bank transfer | 7–14 business days |
| Buy Now Pay Later | BNPL provider handles | 5–10 business days |

**Note**: We cannot process refunds to a different payment method than the one used for purchase. If your card has been cancelled, please contact support before initiating a return.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following cases:

- Item is returned in a condition that reduces its resale value.
- Only part of the order is returned.
- Items from a bundle are returned separately.
- Promotional discounts are recalculated on the returned item.

## Shipping Cost Refunds

- **Original shipping costs** are refunded only if the return is due to our error (wrong item or damaged goods).
- **Expedited shipping fees** are non-refundable unless ShopEase is at fault.
- **Return shipping costs** are covered by us for defective or damaged items; otherwise, the customer is responsible.

## Double Charge Disputes

If you believe you were charged twice for the same order:

1. Check your bank or payment provider — some pending charges drop off within 3–5 business days.
2. If both charges cleared, contact us at **billing@shopease.com** with your order number.
3. We will investigate and issue a refund for the duplicate charge within **2 business days** of confirmation.

## Refund Timeline After Return

1. We receive your returned item.
2. Our team inspects it within **1–3 business days**.
3. Refund is approved or disputed.
4. Refund is issued within **5–10 business days** (depending on your payment method).

Total estimated time from return drop-off to refund: **7–14 business days**.

## Refunds for Lost Packages

If your tracked shipment shows "delivered" but you never received it:

1. Wait **3 business days** — occasionally carriers mark deliveries early.
2. Check with neighbors or building reception.
3. Contact your carrier with the tracking number.
4. If still unresolved, contact ShopEase at **support@shopease.com** within **30 days of the reported delivery date**.
5. We will open a carrier investigation.
6. If the investigation confirms the package was lost, we will issue a **full refund or replacement** (your choice).

## Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will **not** be issued for:

- Digital or downloadable products once accessed.
- Perishable goods after delivery.
- Personalized items unless defective.
- Items returned outside the return window.
- Returns not meeting condition requirements.
- Shipping costs for customer-initiated returns (except in special circumstances).

## Abuse Policy

ShopEase reserves the right to limit or deny return and refund requests for accounts with a history of excessive returns or suspected abuse.
