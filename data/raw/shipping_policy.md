# Shipping Policy

## Overview

ShopEase offers several shipping options to meet your delivery timeline and budget.

## Shipping Methods and Estimated Delivery Times

| Shipping Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|----------------|-------------------|------|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days | Free over $35; $5.99 otherwise |
| Expedited Shipping | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight Shipping | Next business day | $24.99 |
| International Standard | 10–21 business days | Varies by region |
| International Express | 5–10 business days | Varies by region |

**Note**: Delivery times are estimates and may vary during peak seasons.

## Order Processing Time

- Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST Monday–Friday** are processed the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST or on **weekends/holidays** are processed the next business day.
- Orders for personalized or made-to-order items may require 3–7 additional business days.

## Tracking Your Order

- You will receive a **shipping confirmation email** within 24 hours of dispatch, including a tracking number.
- Use the tracking number on the carrier's website or in your ShopEase account under **Orders > Track Order**.
- Tracking updates may take up to **24 hours** after dispatch to appear.

## Shipping Delays

Common reasons for shipping delays:

- **Weather disruptions**: Severe weather delays are beyond our control. We will notify you if delays exceed 48 hours.
- **High-volume periods**: Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and the holiday season (mid-November to January) may see delays of 1–3 business days.
- **Carrier issues**: Occasionally, carrier capacity constraints cause delays. We monitor these closely.
- **Address issues**: Incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses can cause delivery failures. ShopEase is not liable for non-delivery due to address errors.

## Lost or Missing Shipments

If your package appears lost:

1. Confirm the delivery address in your order.
2. Wait **3 business days** after the expected delivery date.
3. Check with neighbors or building management.
4. Contact the carrier with your tracking number.
5. If still unresolved, file a claim with ShopEase at **support@shopease.com** within **30 days** of expected delivery.

## Delivered But Not Received

If the carrier marks a package as "delivered" but you have not received it:

- This sometimes happens when packages are delivered to a neighbor or building reception.
- Check all delivery locations at your address.
- If not found, contact us immediately. We will file a carrier investigation.
- Resolution may take 5–10 business days.

## International Shipping

- ShopEase ships to **over 50 countries**.
- **Customs, duties, and taxes** are the responsibility of the recipient. These fees are not included in the shipping charge.
- ShopEase is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing.
- Some products cannot be shipped internationally due to trade restrictions.

## Shipping Restrictions

Certain items **cannot be shipped** to specific locations:

- Lithium batteries: restricted on international flights in some regions.
- Aerosol products: restricted for air shipping.
- Certain electronics: may require export licenses.
