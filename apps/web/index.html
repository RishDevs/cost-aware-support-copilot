Cost-Aware Routing Logic

Used when: High retrieval confidence (>0.75) + Low complexity + Low risk intent (FAQ, general info)

Tier B — Balanced

Used when: Medium risk (returns, shipping, warranty) or moderate complexity
Top-K: 4–6 chunks · Max tokens: 400

Model: GPT-4o
Used when: High risk (refunds, payment), low retrieval confidence, multi-intent queries, quality SLA mode
Top-K: 6–8 chunks · Max tokens: 700
